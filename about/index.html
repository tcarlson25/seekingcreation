ABOUT US

we're so happy you're here!

Hi! We are Meagan and Tyler – two Texas natives with huge hearts for exploring. We met at Texas A&M University while ironically on a trip to Ireland. We have had a passion for seeing new places since our very first date. We often intentionally surpass the latest and greatest possession, to give gifts of experiences and go on dates full of adventure. Fast forward to married life, where we now both work full time- Meagan as a travel nurse and Tyler as a software engineer. Even with our jobs, we do our best to prioritize our weekends and days off with planning and travel. We hope this page will inspire you to get outside, explore new destinations, and find joy in seeking His creation.
